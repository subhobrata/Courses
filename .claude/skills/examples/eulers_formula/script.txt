# Euler's Formula - Video Script

## Total Duration: ~40 seconds

---

## Introduction (0-4s)
Euler's formula has been called the most beautiful equation in mathematics, and today, we're going to see why.

## The Formula (4-10s)
Here it is: e to the power of i times pi, plus one, equals zero. Five fundamental constants, all connected in one elegant expression.

## Complex Plane (11-16s)
To understand this, we need to venture into the complex plane. Here, the horizontal axis represents real numbers, and the vertical axis represents imaginary numbers.

## Unit Circle (16-20s)
And here's the unit circle. This is where the magic happens.

## The Journey (20-30s)
When we start at e to the power of i times zero, we're at the point one on the real axis. But as we increase the angle, traveling around the unit circle, we trace out a beautiful path. And when we reach an angle of pi radians, we land exactly at negative one.

## The Connection (30-35s)
So e to the power of i times pi equals negative one. Add one to both sides, and we get our famous equation: e to the power of i times pi, plus one, equals zero.

## Conclusion (35-40s)
A simple equation that connects five of the most important numbers in mathematics. That's the beauty of Euler's formula.

- 0-4s: Title animation and introduction
- 4-10s: Show and emphasize the formula

- 16-20s: Draw the unit circle
- 20-30s: Animate the journey from 1 to -1 around the circle
- 30-35s: Show the algebraic transformation
- 35-40s: Final emphasis on the complete formula
